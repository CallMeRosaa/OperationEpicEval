# ADR-0004: Success is measured in time returned to the mission

**Status:** Proposed

**Context:** Evaluations and awards are valuable. Their outcomes shape careers and recognize performance. The **process** around them is not: drafting from memory, emailing PDFs, marking them up, saving, and emailing them back through every layer. By our estimate, 60–80% of that work happens right at the deadline.

**Decision:** Every feature is judged by how much non-value-added time it removes from creating, coordinating, evaluating, editing and routing. The outcome (a strong, accurate record) must stay the same or get better.

**Implications:**
- **No email or PDF loop.** Review, comments and edits happen in place, with one live version and a full history.
- **Always nearly ready.** The journal and AI coach mean a draft exists before the quarter ends. At the start of a cycle, the AI can assemble a first draft from the member's entries for them to edit.
- **Front-load the work.** Suspense tracking nudges early, and dashboards show leaders what is at risk before the deadline, not after.
- **Meet people where they are.** Existing PDF/Word drafts can be imported. Export produces the official format at the end.
- **Measure it.** Instrument time in each step from day one so the pilot can prove hours returned.
