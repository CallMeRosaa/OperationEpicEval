# ADR-0005: The project is also a DAF speed-to-capability pathfinder

**Status:** Accepted (2026-09-29)

**Context:** Besides delivering the product, the proposer wants to learn how fast the DAF can take an identified gap to an MVP and IOC in an IL4/IL5 environment under current processes, technology and regulations. That covers cyber, comm/IT, software factories, the AI Factory, privacy/legal, CFMs, MAJCOM/HQ and DAF/A1.

**Decision:** Move as fast as policy allows and instrument every step. Log each approval, handoff, wait and blocker in `docs/pathfinder/benchmark-log.csv` as it happens, with owner and duration. Deliver a benchmark report and a playbook at IOC.

**Consequences:** Throughput metrics (days to environment, pipeline, ATO/cATO, MVP, IOC) sit beside the mission metrics. Logging starts with the first AI Factory meeting, not after the build.
