Awards Ecosystem Blueprint

One secure workspace where Airmen log what they do all year, every unit's award rules are codified, and nomination packages move up the chain with AI help at every step. This page uses the 96th Maintenance Squadron as the worked example.

How it fits together

Three layers, one assistant

Capture

The journal

Members jot down jobs, efforts, dates, impact and metrics as they happen. The notes feed any quarterly, semi-annual or annual package.

Context

The unit rulebook

The SEL maps flights, sections and elements, then sets who can apply for each award, its format, routing and suspense dates. Setup takes 5–10 minutes.

Coordination

The routing chain

Packages move from the member through supervisors and flight leadership to the SEL and board, with comments, tracked edits and returns.

AI assistantSets up rules from the SOP · coaches the member · scores the writing against the rubric · suggests edits · helps supervisors review. It is advisory only and never scores people for the board.
Context layer · worked example

96 MXS organization

Select any flight, section or element to see which awards a member assigned there can apply for, and how their package routes. Eligibility stacks: a PGM member sees Production Section, Munitions Flight and squadron awards.

Award names and formats are placeholders until the unit's SOPs are loaded. "Flight (confirm name)" under Systems needs confirming, and PMEL sections are still to be mapped.
